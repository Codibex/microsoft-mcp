# v0.8.0 - Teams meeting transcripts and insights

## Highlights

- Adds four read-only Teams tools: `teams_list_meeting_transcripts`,
  `teams_read_meeting_transcript`, `teams_list_meeting_insights` and
  `teams_read_meeting_insight`
- Adds transcript metadata/VTT content plus AI meeting insights with notes,
  subpoints, action items and mentions
- Adds structured `meeting-transcript-not-found` and
  `meeting-insight-not-found` errors, bounded `top` values and safe content
  truncation
- Unifies Graph failures across all servers through `GraphServiceException`
  while preserving actionable error codes and `Next:` hints

## Upgrade notes

- Add the delegated permissions `OnlineMeetingTranscript.Read.All` and
  `OnlineMeetingAiInsight.Read.All` in addition to the existing Teams
  permissions, then consent again if needed.
- Meeting insights require a Microsoft 365 Copilot license and tenant-side
  Graph access. Transcripts and insights are available for work or school
  accounts only and may take time to appear after a meeting.
- No changes are required to existing `TenantId`/`ClientId` configuration.
